Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install any software to use SyncTown?

No. SyncTown runs entirely in your browser—just open the link and start creating. It works on desktop, tablet, and most mobile browsers.

Will real-time sync lag or glitch?

SyncTown uses low-latency WebSocket and CRDT tech; edits are pushed in milliseconds and auto-saved. No need to smash refresh or worry about losing work.

How is my project's privacy protected?

Rooms are private by default—only invited users can join. Audio is transferred over HTTPS and stored encrypted. You can delete or download your data at any time.

Why SyncTown?

We built SyncTown so anyone—from seasoned producers to first-timers—can jump into a browser, claim a personal track, and create together without juggling plug-ins or bouncing stems. Real-time, zero-lag syncing replaces clunky file-sharing with an experience that feels as immediate as jamming in the same room.
